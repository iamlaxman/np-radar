Privacy Policy

How NP Radar handles your data — transparent, minimal, and privacy-first.

Last updated: June 7, 2026

🔒

TL;DR: NP Radar does not collect personal data, does not use tracking cookies, does not require accounts, and does not store your search history. Domain lookups are processed in real-time and are not permanently stored. We don't sell data because we don't have any.

1. Overview

NP Radar ("we," "our," or "us") is a free domain intelligence platform accessible at npradar.laxman-poudel.com.np. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

NP Radar is designed to be privacy-first. We do not require user accounts, we do not collect personal information, we do not use tracking cookies, and we do not store your domain search history. The service operates as a stateless lookup tool — you enter a domain, we fetch public data, and display it. That's it.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Domain Lookups

When you search for a domain, we process that domain name on our server to fetch publicly available information including WHOIS records, DNS records, SSL certificates, and IP geolocation data. Domain queries may be temporarily cached in server memory for performance optimization (typically 5 minutes) but are not permanently stored, logged, or associated with any user.

2.2 Server Logs

Like most web servers, our hosting infrastructure may generate temporary access logs that include IP addresses, request timestamps, and URLs accessed. These logs are used solely for:

  • Debugging technical issues
  • Monitoring server performance
  • Preventing abuse and rate limiting
  • Ensuring service availability

Server logs are rotated regularly and are not used for tracking, profiling, or advertising purposes. We do not combine log data with any other information sources.

2.3 Rate Limiting

To prevent abuse and ensure fair usage, we implement basic rate limiting based on IP addresses. This data is stored only in memory and is automatically cleared within 60 seconds. It is never written to disk or used for any other purpose.

3. How We Use Information

The limited information processed by our server is used exclusively for:

  • Providing the service: Fetching and displaying domain intelligence data
  • Performance optimization: Temporary in-memory caching of lookup results
  • Abuse prevention: Rate limiting to protect server resources
  • Debugging: Identifying and fixing technical issues

We do not use any information for advertising, marketing, user profiling, or any commercial purpose. NP Radar is a free, community-driven tool.

4. Cookies & Tracking

NP Radar does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. We do not set any third-party cookies. The only cookie we may use is a minimal functional cookie (stored locally in your browser via localStorage) to remember your preferred tab selection on the results page. This data never leaves your browser and is not transmitted to our server.

We do not use:

  • Google Analytics or any analytics platform
  • Facebook Pixel or social media trackers
  • Advertising networks or ad cookies
  • Fingerprinting or device identification
  • Any form of user tracking

5. Third-Party Services

To provide domain intelligence data, our backend server queries the following external services. When we make these queries, the domain name and/or IP address being investigated is transmitted as part of the request:

Service Purpose Data Sent
register.com.np .NP WHOIS lookups Domain name
RDAP.org WHOIS for non-.NP domains Domain name

ip-api.com / ipapi.co IP geolocation IP address
HackerTarget API Reverse IP lookup IP address

Please refer to the respective privacy policies of these services for details on how they handle data. NP Radar does not control or have access to how these third parties process the information we send them.

6. Data Security

NP Radar is designed to be stateless — we do not maintain a database of users, searches, or personal information. This architecture inherently limits the risk of data exposure. Additionally:

  • All traffic between your browser and our server can be served over HTTPS
  • Rate limiting prevents automated abuse and scraping
  • Server access is restricted and monitored
  • No user data is stored on disk — only temporary in-memory caching

7. Data Retention

Domain lookup results are cached in server memory for approximately 5 minutes to improve performance for repeated queries. After this period, the cache is automatically cleared.

Server logs (if any) are rotated on a daily or weekly basis depending on the hosting environment. We do not retain logs for extended periods.

Rate limiting counters are stored in memory and reset every 60 seconds.

8. Children's Privacy

NP Radar is a technical tool intended for domain administrators, web developers, security researchers, and digital agencies. It is not directed at children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through our service, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

9. Your Rights

Since NP Radar does not collect, store, or process personal data, most data subject rights (such as access, rectification, or deletion requests under GDPR or similar regulations) are not applicable. However, if you have any concerns:

  • You can use the service without providing any personal information
  • Domain lookups are anonymous and not tied to your identity
  • No account creation or login is required
  • You can contact us with any privacy-related questions

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal compliance. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Since we do not collect contact information, we encourage users to review this policy periodically.

Material changes will be announced via our website or other appropriate channels.
